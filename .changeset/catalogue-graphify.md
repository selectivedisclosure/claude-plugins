First release. The catalogue lists its first plugin, `selectivedisclosure-graphify`, from [selectivedisclosure/claude-graphify-plugin](https://github.com/selectivedisclosure/claude-graphify-plugin). From this release on, the catalogue carries its own version in `metadata.version` of `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json`. It moves when an entry is added, removed or repointed, never when a catalogued plugin releases.
